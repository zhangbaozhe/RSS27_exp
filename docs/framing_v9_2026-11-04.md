that the user confirmed through grill decisions Q1–Q40. The decisions are

## What changed in `AGENTS.md`

- **Framing.** It now points to v9 instead of v8. v8 and its predecessors are
  historical. The 10/28 and 10/31 dev checks were absent from this checkout and
  must be regenerated at the new pin before any of their numbers is used.
- **Project description.** The new title, the three-factor central hypothesis,
  and the two scenes: the 2D three-doorway passage and the UR5e two-cabinet
  transfer, each with four tasks. K ∈ {8, 16, 32} in 2D and {6, 12} for the
  arm.
- **Repository table.** The paper repository is written as `<paper>/`. The
  `experiments/` package is created only after the re-pin. `<paper>/main.tex`
  is edited only on an explicit request.
- **pivopt pin.** The user approved a re-pin to pivopt master (`01654104` when
  the decision was made). The equivalence tests compare migrated legacy
  configurations against reference outputs generated at `94f7c1cd`, because
  master removed `FixedTotalTime`. Every new experiment states all optimizer
  settings explicitly. softplus exists only on master.
- **Protocol.**
  - The v9 §9 development and held-out split replaces the v8 split and its 36
    held-out cells.
  - Constants that are fixed on development data are listed explicitly.
  - The freeze now also covers the instance generators, the decision rules and
    the frozen encoder.
  - Statistical units are defined: seeds for training runs; nominal-plan
    instances, clustered by configuration, for the Prediction.
  - A run with more than 10% clipped gradient steps now also counts as an
    estimator failure.
- **Experiment code.** Training uses SGD with momentum 0.9 and a zero-initialized
  head. The list of required derivative checks and the vocabulary now follow
  v9 §3–§4.
- **Paper-facing rules.**
  - Diff²'s inputs are spelled out: depth images with a ResNet18 encoder,
    frame-local occupancy and ESDF, polytopes built from occupancy, and
    receding horizon.
  - ESDF losses are excluded. The outer clearance loss uses analytic distances
    to obstacle primitives.
  - Global occupancy or voxel inputs with the small coordinate-channel CNN are
    allowed.
