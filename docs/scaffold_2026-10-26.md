## Evidence boundaries and remaining research

Long training, calibration searches and formal statistical studies are not part
of this scaffold. Existing `agent_docs/...` references in imported docstrings
refer to the source repository at the recorded commit. The source maintainer
tutorials and retired adaptive scripts are excluded.

Historical raw datasets/checkpoints are not included. In particular, the old
`research/perturbation-1020` tag and documented `build/perturbation_*` artifacts
were not available in the source checkout inspected for this task. The
certified driver's `reproduce` command requires those original artifacts.

The complete paper protocol still needs explicit decisions and implementation
for grouped timing, continuous local D profiles, residual normalization,
backward-target alignment, no-argmin attribution and the C-K reference. None of
those scientific or numerical choices is changed by this migration.
